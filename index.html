Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

1. Our promise

Luma is built privacy-first. Your health data belongs to you: we don't sell it, we don't share it with advertisers and we don't use it to profile you.

2. What is stored on your device

Your profile (goal, cycle and period length), periods, daily logs (flow, symptoms, moods, energy, sleep, water, intimacy, notes) and app settings. Everything is kept in a local database encrypted with AES-256; the encryption key is stored in your device's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore). You don't need an account to use the app.

3. No ads, no trackers

The app contains no advertising or third-party tracking SDKs. Health data is never sent to analytics services. If optional, anonymous usage analytics are ever introduced, they will be off by default and enabled only with your consent.

4. Optional cloud backup

If you connect an Apple or Google account, a copy of your data is stored in your private cloud space (Google Firebase), accessible only to your account and protected by access rules. With "Keep notes & intimacy on this device" turned on, those two fields are never uploaded. Signing out keeps your data on the device.

5. Partner mode

You choose exactly what a partner can see: current phase and period status, predicted period date, fertile window and daily mood. Personal notes, intimacy entries and symptoms are never shared. You can turn each item off at any time.

6. AI Cycle Coach

The coach currently runs entirely on your device. If a cloud-based assistant is offered in the future, only an anonymised summary (such as cycle day and symptom names) will be sent — never your notes, intimacy entries or identity — and only after you agree.

7. Notifications & home widget

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. In discreet mode (on by default) notification and widget texts never mention periods, fertility or health.

8. Your rights (KVKK / GDPR)

You can access, export (JSON), import and permanently delete all your data at any time, free of charge, in Profile → Account & backup. "Delete all my data and account" first deletes your cloud backup and account, then everything on this device. Under KVKK (Law No. 6698) and the GDPR you also have the right to request information and correction and to object to processing.

9. Payments

Subscriptions are processed by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your card details; we only learn whether your subscription is active.

10. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13.

11. Changes & contact

We'll announce significant changes to this policy in the app. For privacy, data deletion and support requests, contact us at lumacycleapp@gmail.com.

Tıbbi sorumluluk reddi ve tahmin yöntemi

Son güncelleme: 9 Kasım 2026

Luma tıbbi bir cihaz veya doğum kontrol yöntemi değildir. Tahminler istatistiksel kestirimlerdir ve gebeliği önlemek için kullanılmamalıdır. Sağlığınızla ilgili konularda her zaman bir sağlık profesyoneline danışın.

1. Bir sonraki reglin nasıl tahmin edilir

Geçmiş döngülerinin her birinin uzunluğunu (bir regl başlangıcından bir sonrakine kadar) ölçeriz. Bir sonraki reglin, son 6 geçerli döngünün ağırlıklı ortalamasıyla tahmin edilir; yakın tarihli döngüler daha çok etkiler. Henüz döngü kaydın yoksa kurulumda girdiğin döngü süresi (varsayılan 28 gün) kullanılır.

2. Olağandışı döngülerin ayıklanması

21 günden kısa veya 45 günden uzun döngüler ile (en az 3 döngün olduğunda) medyanından %30'dan fazla sapan döngüler aykırı değer kabul edilir. Tek bir olağandışı ay tahminlerini bozmasın diye ortalamaya katılmazlar, ancak düzenlilik değerlendirmeni düşürürler.

3. Ovulasyon ve doğurganlık penceresi

Ovulasyon, beklenen bir sonraki reglden yaklaşık 14 gün önce tahmin edilir; çünkü ovulasyon sonrası luteal faz oldukça sabittir (yaklaşık 10–16 gün). Doğurganlık penceresi ovulasyondan önceki 5 günü, ovulasyon gününü ve sonraki günü kapsar: sperm yaklaşık 5 güne kadar, yumurta ise yaklaşık 12–24 saat yaşayabilir.

4. Yüzde değil, seviye

Hamile kalma olasılığın Düşük, Orta veya Yüksek olarak gösterilir. Kesin yüzdeler, takvim tabanlı bir yöntemin sunamayacağı bir kesinlik izlenimi verir; bu yüzden bilerek göstermiyoruz.

5. Belirsizlik ve düzenlilik

Tahmini tarihler, döngü uzunluklarının standart sapması kadar bir aralıkla gösterilir (yeterli veri olana kadar ±2 gün, en fazla ±7 gün). Bu sapma en fazla 2 günse döngün düzenli, 5 güne kadarsa biraz düzensiz, daha fazlaysa düzensiz kabul edilir.

6. Geciken regl

Reglin tahmin edilen günde başlamadıysa uygulama kaç gün geciktiğini gösterir ve başlamış gibi davranmak yerine bir sonraki tahmini ileri kaydırır. Stres, hastalık, seyahat, kilo değişimi, emzirme, hormonal doğum kontrolü veya gebelik döngüyü kaydırabilir.

7. Sınırlamalar

Takvim tabanlı tahminler düzensiz döngülerde, hormonal doğum kontrolünü bıraktıktan sonra, doğum sonrasında, emzirirken, perimenopozda ve PKOS veya tiroid bozuklukları gibi durumlarda daha az güvenilirdir. Uygulama vücut ısısı, hormon testi veya başka biyobelirteç kullanmaz.

8. Ne zaman doktora danışmalısın

Döngülerin düzenli olarak 21 günden kısa veya 35 günden uzunsa, gebe değilken 90 gündür regl olmadıysan, kanama 7 günden uzun sürüyor ya da çok yoğunsa, regl arasında veya ilişki sonrası kanaman oluyorsa veya ağrı günlük yaşamını engelliyorsa lütfen bir sağlık profesyoneline başvur.

Medical disclaimer & how predictions work

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Luma is not a medical device and not a method of birth control. Its predictions are statistical estimates and must not be used to prevent pregnancy. Always consult a healthcare professional about your health.

1. How your next period is predicted

We measure the length of each of your past cycles (from one period start to the next). Your next period is predicted from a weighted average of your last 6 valid cycles — recent cycles count more. Until you have logged cycles, the cycle length you entered during setup is used (28 days by default).

2. Filtering unusual cycles

Cycles shorter than 21 or longer than 45 days — and, once you have at least 3 cycles, cycles more than 30% away from your median — are treated as outliers. They are left out of the average so one unusual month doesn't skew your predictions, but they do lower your regularity rating.

3. Ovulation & fertile window

Ovulation is estimated about 14 days before your next expected period, because the luteal phase (after ovulation) is fairly constant at roughly 10–16 days. The fertile window covers the 5 days before ovulation, the day of ovulation and the day after: sperm can survive up to about 5 days, an egg about 12–24 hours.

4. Levels, not percentages

Your chance of pregnancy is shown as Low, Medium or High. Exact percentages would suggest a precision that a calendar-based method can't offer, so we deliberately don't show them.

5. Uncertainty & regularity

Predicted dates come with a range of ± the standard deviation of your cycle lengths (±2 days until there is enough data, at most ±7). Your cycle is rated regular when this variation is up to 2 days, somewhat irregular up to 5 days, and irregular above that.

6. Late periods

If your period hasn't started on the predicted day, the app shows how many days late it is and moves the next prediction forward instead of assuming it has started. Stress, illness, travel, weight changes, breastfeeding, hormonal contraception or pregnancy can all shift a cycle.

7. Limitations

Calendar-based estimates are less reliable with irregular cycles, after stopping hormonal contraception, after giving birth, while breastfeeding, during perimenopause and with conditions such as PCOS or thyroid disorders. The app doesn't use temperature, hormone tests or other biomarkers.

8. When to talk to a doctor

Please see a healthcare professional if your cycles are regularly shorter than 21 or longer than 35 days, you haven't had a period for 90 days and aren't pregnant, bleeding lasts more than 7 days or is very heavy, you bleed between periods or after sex, or pain keeps you from your daily activities.
